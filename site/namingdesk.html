Welcome to the Naming Desk

Ratchet & Clank shipped with no names in its code. Thousands of functions and variables are still called things like FUN_001f96f8 or unk7C, and each real name has to be found by hand.

You can help. Pick a name, watch what it does in PCSX2 and suggest a better one. Each suggestion becomes a ready-to-apply GitHub issue.

This is a preview, so things may still change. Spotted a bug or have an idea? Use Report a bug at the top of the page.
